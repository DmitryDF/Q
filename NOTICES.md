## What is deliberately **not** here

The system was built against a private knowledge library containing full-text
extractions of the works it draws on. **Those extractions are not in this repository
and are not distributed.** They are the authors' copyright.

This is why every knowledge-library citation in the skills reads
`<KL>/<Domain>/<book-slug>` rather than a real path: the skill tells you *which
book and which chapter* backs its judgment, and expects you to supply your own
lawfully-obtained copy.

Each affected skill states a fallback for an unreachable source and marks its
output `UNVERIFIED` rather than pretending the grounding was there. The judgment is
weaker without the books; the skills say so.

## Anthropic documentation

`rules/prompt-engineering.md` is a synthesis of Anthropic's public prompt-engineering documentation. It reproduces fenced prompt-template blocks verbatim alongside quoted guidance sentences. Its sources are listed with URLs at the top of that file, and each section carries an `(S1)`-style marker naming which one it came from.

These are published prompt snippets, offered by Anthropic for use.

Several other rules files cite Anthropic engineering posts and documentation by URL
with short quotations: *Building Effective Agents*, *Writing Effective Tools for
Agents*, *Demystifying Evals*, the *Safe Agents Framework*, and the sub-agents and
tool-use documentation.
